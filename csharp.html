C# Projects

Chess Game Viewer

Made this after learning about arrays and 2d arrays. It does not play like chess, but the pieces can be moved around and “captured”. The state of the chess board can be saved and loaded in.

The scenario was that a high school chess tournament was taking place, and the judges needed software to track player moves and display it to the audience.

Tic-Tac-Toe

Another array problem. This one was really fun and was actually one of the first things I tried doing when starting classes. At the time I was in over my head, but by the time it came and tic tac toe was assigned as a project I felt more than ready. I even made a 'bot' that you play against, but it is not very good at trying to win

Budget Tool

The final project for my Intro to Programming class, the budget tool. I made this for my girlfriend to use at her work, because she has to handle budgeting for her clients and it was previously all done on paper, which was an organizational mess. The goal was to digitize all of her work and streamline it to her work desktop.
